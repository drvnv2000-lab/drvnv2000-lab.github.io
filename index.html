Do a patient's drugs help predict kidney injury in the ICU?

A pre-registered study of 2,520 ICU stays from 186 US hospitals. Adding five drugs improved a pre-specified model, but only when drugs were counted from their codes: a name search misses two in three piperacillin-tazobactam exposures.

+0.036 AUROC

95% CI 0.006 to 0.071; no gain in a flexible model, reported as such

How I work

  • The analysis plan is committed before any model is trained, and can only grow: a test fails on any commit that edits it.
  • Validation is grouped by patient, so repeat admissions never leak between training and testing.
  • Every number in a write-up is tested against the result file it came from.
  • Limits and corrections are published, including the uncomfortable ones.
